This is a standard paragraph element used for blocks of text.

Bold text: Visually thick letters without extra importance.

Italic text: Visually slanted text used for alternate voice or mood.

Underlined text: Puts a horizontal line under the text.

Small text: Renders the font one size smaller for disclaimers.

Strong text: Important text that renders as bold.

Emphasized text: Stressed text that renders as italics.

Marked text: Highlighted text with a yellow background.
